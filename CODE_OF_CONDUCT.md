# Code of Conduct

## Our pledge

We want bugcap to be a welcoming project for everyone, whatever their experience, background or
identity. We pledge to make participation harassment-free.

## Expected behaviour

- Be respectful and patient, especially with newcomers.
- Give and accept feedback on the work, not the person.
- Assume good faith, and ask when something is unclear.
- Keep discussion on topic and constructive.

## Unacceptable behaviour

- Harassment, insults, personal attacks, or discriminatory language or jokes
- Sexualised language or imagery, or unwelcome attention
- Trolling, deliberate derailing, or repeated off-topic noise
- Publishing someone's private information without permission
- Anything else that would reasonably be considered inappropriate in a professional setting

## Scope

This applies to all project spaces (issues, pull requests, discussions, reviews) and to public
spaces when someone is representing the project.

## Enforcement

Maintainers may edit, hide or remove comments, close or lock threads, and temporarily or
permanently ban people who break these rules. To report a problem, contact the maintainer
privately through the contact details on their GitHub profile
([@amenalahassa](https://github.com/amenalahassa)). Reports are handled confidentially, and the
reporter will not be retaliated against.
